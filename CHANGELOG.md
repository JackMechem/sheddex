# Changelog

sheddex uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): **MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH**. Bump PATCH for fixes,
MINOR for new features, MAJOR for 1.0 and anything that breaks how people use it. The version lives
in `apps/web/package.json` (shown under the sidebar logo); it's **beta** until 1.0
(`apps/web/lib/version.ts`).

## 0.9.0 — beta (2026-10-17)

The first public beta.

- **Practice tools:** Metronome (with changing meters), Polyrhythm Metric Modulation metronome,
  Tempo Trainer, Tuner, Note / Scale / Interval Trainers with mic listen modes, Practice Timer,
  Recorder (with a tune's song and a mixer), Slow Downer.
- **Play along:** Jam Practice, Bass Genius (walking bass, chords and drums over your charts, bass
  players learned from transcriptions), Drum Generator (32 styles), Random Sticking Warmup,
  Syncopation.
- **Ear training:** Guess the Interval, Chord and Progression, with levelled Learn mode.
- **Library:** Tunes I Know / Tunes to Learn, setlists, chord charts (iReal Pro import, editor,
  playlists).
- **Shed Log:** practice calendar, routines, day timer, goals, streaks, progress cards.
- **Community:** profiles, follows, posts (tunes, setlists, routines, milestones).
- **Lessons:** teachers and students, a page per lesson, assignments with hand-ins and feedback,
  lesson notes, booking with calendar invites, all materials, feature settings.
- **Feedback:** a Report a bug button in the corner of every page (closable); report bugs, ideas and anything else from the sidebar's ⋮ menu or /feedback (with
  a screenshot when signed in), and report posts or profiles; see what you've sent and its status.
- Admin: an Analytics tab with customizable charts, based on anonymous daily usage counts (no trackers; off with Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control) and the site's own data. A shorter, clearer privacy policy.
- Admin: Mayday switches (maintenance, Community off, pausing posts, sign-ups, uploads and feedback, a site banner) and flood limits.
- iPhone / iPad: sound plays with silent mode on.
- Link previews: shared sheddex links show a card with the logo.
